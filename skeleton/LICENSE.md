# skeleton — human skeletal anatomy (from UBERON)

A pack of human skeletal anatomy facts derived from the **UBERON anatomy
ontology**, in the learn_me `DeckDocument` interchange format. Each card is a
"X is part of Y" fact: front = the anatomical term, back = its `part_of`
parent in UBERON.
