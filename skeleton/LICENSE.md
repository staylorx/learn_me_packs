# skeleton — human skeletal anatomy (from UBERON + Gray's Anatomy)

An illustrated deck of human skeletal anatomy facts in the learn_me
`DeckDocument` interchange format: the skeleton's `part_of` hierarchy, one fact
per card, with public-domain anatomy plates.
